Reactions is the ring of reaction buttons that appears around a seat on hover: heart, snack and scratch, plus the poke bell when `canPoke`.

Provide `onReact(kind)`; set `canPoke` only for a member who has not acted yet.

- Place it centred on the seat; it is 118px square and the buttons sit just outside its edge.
- Heart, snack and scratch are for anyone at the table. Poke appears only when there is something to poke about.
- The glyphs are 8x8 pixel art. Each button is told apart by shape first; the ring colour is secondary.
